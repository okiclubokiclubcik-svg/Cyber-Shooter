Tactical Directives & Field Instructions
💻 PC Controls:
  • WASD or Arrow Keys to move character in all directions.
  • Mouse cursor to aim crosshair.
  • Left Mouse Button to shoot (hold down for continuous fire).
  • Keys 1, 2, 3, 4 or Mouse Wheel to switch unlocked weapons.
  • P or ESC to pause battle.
📱 Mobile / Touch Controls:
  • Left thumb on left screen half: dynamic virtual movement joystick.
  • Right thumb on FIRE button or right screen half: touch-aim and continuous fire!
  • Bottom weapon dock: tap weapon icons to switch instantly.
⚔️ Waves & Boss Encounters:
  • Survive relentless waves of humanoid cyborg forces.
  • Every 5th Wave (Wave 5, 10, 15...) deploys a lethal Boss! Defeating a Boss showers the arena with huge credit bonuses and full medical drops!
  • Defeated enemies drop Health Pacts (+1 Life), Agility Boosts, 2x Firepower, and Energy Shields.
